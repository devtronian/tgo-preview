Our story

Proven experience, taken overseas

TG Oversea Solutions Recruitment Co., Ltd. was founded on 28 October 2025, building on our sister company T.G. Labor Co., Ltd.

  1. Pre-2025

    Roots in T.G. Labor

    More than five years managing workforces, covering labour law, health and employer coordination.

  2. Oct 2025

    TG Oversea Solutions founded

    A company dedicated to international recruitment, built on worker-centred and transparent principles.

  3. 2026

    Licence T. 1307/2569 granted

    Licensed by the Department of Employment, Ministry of Labour, to recruit for overseas work.

  4. Today

    A network across four regions

    Thai massage and spa specialists placed in Saudi Arabia, Oman, Kuwait, the UAE and Europe, with partner networks in Australia and New Zealand.
