For job seekers

Six steps from applying to your first day

Usually around 2–4 months, depending on the country and your documents.

  1. 1

    Register and talk to us

    Apply online, by phone or at our office. We ask about your experience, explain suitable roles and give you every cost in writing.

    You bring ID card and work history

  2. 2

    Interview and trade test

    Interview with the employer or their agent, plus a skills test to the role's standard. The employer makes the selection; we never promise a place before this step.

    You bring Relevant certificates

  3. 3

    Read and sign your contract

    Your contract names the employer, workplace, pay, hours, housing and days off. It's checked by the Department of Employment, and you can read a Thai version before signing.

    You bring Time to read it properly

  4. 4

    Medical and visa

    A medical at an approved clinic, then the work visa and permit. We handle the filing and keep you updated.

    You bring Passport (we can help)

  5. 5

    Pre-departure training

    The department's orientation: local law, culture, basic language, your rights and where to get help in the destination country.

    You bring Attend the full course

  6. 6

    Start work, with support throughout

    Airport pick-up and housing arranged, welfare check-ins, and a go-between with your employer until the contract ends. Call us directly any time.

    You bring Keep our number with you

Costs

Costs to know before you decide

Exact amounts depend on the country, role and employer. We give them to you in writing, per role, before taking any payment.

  • Recruitment service fee

    Within the department's legal rate

  • Medical exam

    Paid at cost to the clinic

  • Passport and visa

    At official government rates

  • Overseas workers' welfare fund

    As required by law

  • Travel

    Some employers pay; we tell you upfront

What we always do

  • Every cost in writing before you pay anything
  • A company receipt for every payment
  • A Thai version of your contract to read before signing
  • The real employer and workplace, so you can check them
  • A named contact you can reach until your contract ends

What we never do

  • Charge more than the department's legal rates
  • Ask you to pay into anyone's personal account
  • Hold on to your passport or ID card
  • Send you to work on a tourist visa
  • Promise you a place before the employer selects you

FAQ

What people ask before applying

Can't find your answer? Call 083-157-0465

How much does it cost to work abroad?

Costs vary by country and role and must stay within the rates set by the Department of Employment. We give you every item in writing before you decide, and issue a company receipt for every payment.

How long until I can travel?

It depends on the destination, the visa process and how ready your documents are, typically around 2–4 months. We'll give you the timeline for your specific role.

How can I check you're a legal agency?

Check our licence no. T. 1307/2569 on the Department of Employment website (doe.go.th) or call the 1694 hotline.

Can I apply if I've never worked abroad?

Yes. Many roles welcome skilled people going abroad for the first time. We prepare you with language, culture and rules training before you leave.

Do I need to speak English?

It depends on the role and employer. Some need basic English, others focus on skills. We'll tell you the level needed and provide language training before departure.

Which documents do I need to apply?

Thai ID card, house registration, passport, passport photos, education or trade certificate, and an employment reference if you have one. No passport yet? We'll show you how to get one.

Who do I contact if there's a problem at work?

Contact our team directly throughout your contract by phone, email or Facebook. You can also reach the Thai Labour Office or Royal Thai Embassy in that country.

Avoid scams

Check these three things before you apply anywhere

Scammers promise fast jobs abroad without a licence. These checks work for any agency, including us.

  1. Ask for the licence number

    An overseas recruiter must hold a Department of Employment licence. Check the number at doe.go.th.

  2. Call the 1694 hotline

    The department's hotline can confirm whether an agency and its job orders are real.

  3. Pay only against a receipt

    Never pay into a personal account, and get a company receipt for every payment.
