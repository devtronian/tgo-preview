Our story

Proven experience, taken global

TG Oversea Solutions Recruitment Co., Ltd. was founded on 28 October 2025 to open safe, transparent and legal pathways for Thai workers to suitable jobs abroad, building on our sister company T.G. Labor Co., Ltd.

  1. Pre-2025

    Roots in T.G. Labor

    More than five years managing workforces, covering labour law, health and employer coordination.

  2. Oct 2025

    TG Oversea Solutions founded

    A company dedicated to international recruitment, built on worker-centred and transparent principles.

  3. 2026

    Licence T. 1307/2569 granted

    Licensed by the Department of Employment, Ministry of Labour, to recruit for overseas work.

  4. Today

    A network across four regions

    Thai massage and spa specialists placed in Saudi Arabia, Oman, Kuwait, the UAE and Europe, with partner networks in Australia and New Zealand.

Mission and principles

Trust is built in how we work

  • People first

  • Nothing hidden

  • Prepared before take-off

Leadership

  • Managing Director

    Sets direction, owns our standards and leads overseas partnerships.

  • Labour law advisor

    Oversees medical screening and fitness to travel.

  • Recruitment and overseas manager

Licence and legal

Fully licensed. Check us any time.

  • Overseas recruitment licence no. T. 1307/2569 from the Department of Employment
  • Verify us on the department's website or via its 1694 hotline
  • Written contracts and itemised fees within legally permitted rates
  • Every worker completes the department's pre-departure orientation
