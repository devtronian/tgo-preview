For job seekers

Four steps from applying to your first day at work

  1. 01

    Register and screening

    Apply online, by phone or at our office. We interview you, assess your skills, suggest suitable roles and give you the costs in writing.

    You bring: ID card and work history

  2. 02

    Skills test and training

    Trade tests to the employer's standard, plus language, culture and local-law training so you're ready on day one.

    You bring: relevant certificates

  3. 03

    Documents, visa and medical

    We handle your contract, medical at an approved clinic, visa and departure approval from the Department of Employment.

    You bring: passport (we can help)

  4. 04

    Departure and aftercare

    Airport pick-up and housing arranged, welfare check-ins, and a go-between with your employer for the whole contract.

    We stay with you: the whole contract

FAQ

What people ask before applying

Can't find your answer? Call 083-157-0465

How much does it cost to work abroad?

Costs vary by country and role and must stay within the rates set by the Department of Employment. We give you every item in writing before you decide, and issue a company receipt for every payment.

How long until I can travel?

It depends on the destination, the visa process and how ready your documents are, typically around 2–4 months. We'll give you the timeline for your specific role.

Can I apply if I've never worked abroad?

Yes. Many roles welcome skilled people going abroad for the first time. We prepare you with language, culture and rules training before you leave.

Do I need to speak English?

It depends on the role and employer. Some need basic English, others focus on skills. We'll tell you the level needed and provide language training before departure.

Which documents do I need to apply?

Thai ID card, house registration, passport, passport photos, education or trade certificate, and an employment reference if you have one. No passport yet? We'll show you how to get one.

How can I check you're a legal agency?

Check our licence no. T. 1307/2569 on the Department of Employment website (doe.go.th) or call the 1694 hotline.

Who do I contact if there's a problem at work?

Contact our team directly throughout your contract by phone, email or Facebook. You can also reach the Thai Labour Office or Royal Thai Embassy in that country.

Avoid scams

Check these three things before you apply anywhere

Scammers promise fast jobs abroad without a licence. These checks work for any agency, including us.

  1. Ask for the licence number

    An overseas recruiter must hold a Department of Employment licence. Check the number at doe.go.th.

  2. Call the 1694 hotline

    The department's hotline can confirm whether an agency and its job orders are real.

  3. Pay only against a receipt

    Never pay into a personal account, and get a company receipt for every payment.
