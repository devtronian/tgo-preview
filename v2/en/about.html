Proven experience, taken global

TG Oversea Solutions Recruitment Co., Ltd. was founded on 28 October 2025 to open safe, transparent and legal pathways for Thai workers to suitable jobs abroad, building on our sister company T.G. Labor Co., Ltd.

  1. Pre-2025

    Roots in T.G. Labor

    More than five years managing workforces, covering labour law, health and employer coordination.

  2. 28 Oct 2025

    TG Oversea Solutions founded

    A company dedicated to international recruitment, built on worker-centred and transparent principles.

  3. 2026

    Licence T. 1307/2569 granted

    Licensed by the Department of Employment, Ministry of Labour, to recruit for overseas work.

  4. Today

    A network across four regions

    Thai massage and spa specialists placed in Saudi Arabia, Oman, Kuwait, the UAE and Europe, with partner networks in Australia and New Zealand.

Vision

To be the overseas recruitment agency Thai workers and employers worldwide trust most, for its transparency and the care it gives every person.

Mission

  1. Recruit and select quality Thai workers matched to each employer's needs.
  2. Operate legally and transparently, with every step open to verification.
  3. Prepare workers with skills, language and cultural training before departure.
  4. Protect workers' safety and quality of life for the full contract.

Trust is built in how we work

  • People first

    Workers' safety, housing and benefits come first for the whole contract.

  • Nothing hidden

    Costs, conditions and contracts in writing, checkable at every step.

  • International network

    Employers and partner agencies across the Middle East, Australia, New Zealand, Europe and Asia.

  • Experienced leadership

    Backgrounds in labour law, medicine and workforce management.

Leadership

Backgrounds in labour law, medicine and workforce management let us see every side of working abroad.

  • Managing Director

    Sets company direction, owns our standards and leads overseas partnerships.

  • Labour law advisor

    Reviews contracts and procedures against Thai and destination-country law.

  • Medical advisor

    Oversees medical screening and fitness-to-travel standards.

  • Recruitment and overseas manager

    Selects candidates, coordinates with employers and supports workers on contract.
