Can I enquire without overseas experience?+

Yes. Suitability depends on experience, skills, language and employer requirements. Each role has its own criteria.

Which documents should I prepare?+

Start with your passport, identity card, CV and relevant skills certificates. The team will explain additional role and destination requirements.

How do I verify the agency and costs?+

Check the company and licence with the Department of Employment or hotline 1694. Request written costs, terms and contract details before deciding.
