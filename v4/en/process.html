02BEFORE YOU GO

Make room
for good questions.

01Can I enquire without overseas experience?+

Yes. Suitability depends on your skills, language ability, experience and each employer’s requirements. Previous overseas work is not a universal requirement.

02How much English do I need?+

Requirements vary by role, employer and destination. Some roles require basic communication; others require formal test scores. Ask us about the requirements for your chosen route.

03What are the costs and timescales?+

These depend on the role, destination and official processing. Request written cost, contract and timeline details before deciding. Visa approval and departure dates cannot be guaranteed.

04How can I verify the recruitment agency?+

Check the company name and licence T. 1307/2569 with Thailand’s Department of Employment at doe.go.th or the 1694 hotline before paying or sending sensitive documents.
